First time? Activate your account

  1. Receive Invitation — Check your email (and spam folder) for an activation invite from our office.
  2. Activate — Click "Get Started" in the email to create your password and verify your identity.
  3. Complete Setup — Follow the on-screen instructions to finish your account.
  4. Log In — Once activated, use the button above or visit 34992.portal.athenahealth.com.
